Atlas Recovery Voice Agent

Nancy from Alpha Bank — a LiveKit voice agent for inbound consumer calls about past-due accounts. CollectWise take-home submission.

Click the widget button in the bottom-right corner and allow microphone access to start a call. Each visit gets its own private session.
Tip: keep your speaker volume moderate (or use headphones). At high volume Nancy's voice can feed back into the microphone and register as an interruption. Robust acoustic echo handling is a known area for further improvement.

A sample conversation

“Hi, I got a letter about my account. My account number is three zero zero one zero one.” → confirm you are Maria → give SSN last four 7301 → hear the balance → say you can’t pay in full and negotiate a plan or settlement.

Edge cases to try

  • Give a wrong SSN (three wrong attempts locks the call)
  • Say “you have the wrong number” or “this isn’t my debt”
  • Describe financial hardship, or demand a 36-month plan
  • Offer a lowball settlement (the floor is concealed, then disclosed)
  • Ask for a human, or interrupt Nancy mid-sentence
